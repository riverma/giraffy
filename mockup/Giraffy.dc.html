Giraffy
A pocket guide that helps you say hard things kindly, and hear them kindly too.
Nonviolent Communication untangles a hard moment into four parts: what happened, what you feel, what you need, and what you would like to ask. Giraffy walks you through them, one at a time. Privacy first, offline, and here to help you process your feelings and needs.
how Giraffy works
A few concepts.
1 · name your needsEvery feeling points to a need: rest, fairness, closeness. You mark each one met, partly met, or unmet.
2 · write a cardA card is one moment put into words: what happened, what you felt, the need underneath, and a request. It goes to someone, or to yourself.
3 · they do the sameCards come back as heard, I'd love to, let's explore, or I cannot. When a need of yours and a need of theirs depend on each other, they are entangled, and Giraffy shows the thread.
4 · over timeCard by card, need by need, toward a world where everyone's needs are met.
Nothing to memorise. Each of these is explained again where you meet it.

← about
With gratitude.
Giraffy exists because of Marshall B. Rosenberg (1934–2015), who spent his life developing and teaching Nonviolent Communication, the framework this app walks you through, and shared it in his book Nonviolent Communication: A Language of Life.
The giraffe was his symbol for this way of speaking: the land animal with the largest heart.
Marshall stood in a longer lineage of nonviolence, and so does this app: Mahatma Gandhi and the ancient principle of ahimsa, the commitment to non-harm in thought, word, and action; Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., who showed that nonviolence is not passivity but a force for justice and connection; and the Buddha's teachings on compassion, presence, and the roots of suffering, which echo through NVC's attention to what is alive in us.
Giraffy is an independent, free project inspired by their work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Center for Nonviolent Communication. To go deeper, and we hope you do, start with Marshall's book, or cnvc.org.
